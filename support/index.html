Support

Stuck on the island? Start here.

Contact us

Open a ticket on our support tracker. Tell us your device, iOS version and what happened. We reply there, usually within two working days. Please do not post receipts or personal details.

Controls

  • Touch: drag the lower left to move. Hold Bonk to hit the nearest toy, or hold the playfield to aim by hand. Dash dodges; hold it while turning in the kart to drift.
  • Game controller: left stick moves, right stick aims, right trigger bonks, A dashes, Start pauses.
  • Keyboard on iPad: WASD moves, the pointer aims, click bonks, Space dashes, Esc pauses.

The Full Lease

The Full Lease is a one-time purchase. It adds Gutter Goblin and Overtime Intern straight away, and opens the Rent hike and Final demand leases once you earn them. Everything else is free.

I bought it, but it is still locked

Open the Full Lease card on the title screen and tap Restore purchase. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple Account you bought it with. Family Sharing is not available for this purchase.

Refunds

Apple handles refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Common questions

Where is my progress saved?

On your device. Deleting the app deletes your progress. Restoring the device from a backup brings it back.

The game runs slowly on my device

Pause the game and switch off Day, night and lighting, or the 3D world. Closing other apps helps on older devices.

There is no sound

Rent Ruckus follows the silent switch. Turn silent mode off and check the music and effects sliders in the pause menu.
