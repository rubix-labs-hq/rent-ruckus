Rent Ruckus

Privacy

Rent Ruckus collects no data. It has no accounts, analytics, ads, tracking or network features.

Effective 23 November 2026. This policy covers the Rent Ruckus app for iPhone and iPad, published by Rubix Labs.

What stays on your device

Your settings, unlocked heroes, trophies, workshop fixtures, receipts and best run are saved on your device so they are there next time. They never leave it. We cannot see them. Deleting the app deletes them. If you use iCloud or device backups, Apple may include this app data in your backup under Apple's terms.

Purchases

The optional Full Lease is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment and your Apple Account. The app only asks the App Store whether the Full Lease is owned on this Apple Account. We receive no payment details, name or email from the purchase. Apple sends us anonymous, aggregated sales reports.

What we do not do

  • No sign-in or account.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or advertising software.
  • No tracking across apps or websites, and no advertising identifier.
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone or photo access.
  • No data sold or shared with anyone.

Children

Because the app collects no personal information, it collects none from children either.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log your IP address and browser details when you visit, under the GitHub Privacy Statement. The site uses no cookies or analytics of its own.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Questions go to the support page.
